FAQ

Common Questions.

Answers to the questions we're asked most often. Can't find what you need? Just ask.

What does Turing Point currently offer?+

Two active services: Websites and AI Automation. A third offering, Digital Services (social media, branding, SEO), is currently in development and marked "Coming Soon" — it isn't available to purchase yet.

How much does a website cost?+

Websites start from ₹8,000. The final price depends on the number of pages, functionality, integrations and overall scope — see the Pricing page for details, or get in touch for a specific quote.

How is AI Automation priced?+

AI Automation is custom-priced. Every workflow is different, so we scope the solution and the cost around your specific business requirements before quoting.

How long does a typical project take?+

Timelines depend on scope and how quickly content and feedback come back to us. We'll give you a clear estimate during the Discover/Plan stage, before work begins — see the Process page for how a project moves from idea to launch.

Do you offer SEO or social media management right now?+

Not yet. Those fall under Digital Services, which is still being built and isn't active. We don't offer or claim advanced SEO or social media services today.

How do I start a project?+

Fill in the enquiry form on the Contact page with a few details about your business and what you need, or reach out directly on WhatsApp or email. We'll follow up to understand your requirements and put together a scope and quote.

Is the pricing on this site final?+

No — pricing shown on the site is an indicative starting point, not a guaranteed final price. Every quote is based on the actual scope of your project.

Can I see examples of past work?+

Yes — the Work page has case studies of projects we've built, including Trinetram Architects and VASTRO.

Still have a question?

Reach out and we'll get back to you directly.
