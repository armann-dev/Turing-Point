This is a general terms template written for a small digital studio. It is provided as a starting point, not legal advice — have a qualified professional review and adapt it to your business before relying on it.

1. Who this applies to

These terms apply to anyone who engages Turing Point ("we", "us") for website or AI automation work, or who submits an enquiry through this site.

2. Our services

We currently offer two active services: Websites and AI Automation. A third offering, Digital Services, is in development and is not available for purchase. The scope of any project is agreed in writing (email or a proposal document) before work begins.

3. Quotes and pricing

Pricing shown on this site is an indicative starting point, not a fixed or guaranteed final price. A final quote is based on the actual scope, pages, features, integrations and timeline of your project, and will be confirmed with you before work starts.

4. Payments

Payment terms (deposit, milestones, final payment) will be agreed with you individually for each project before work begins. Work will not commence, and final files/access will not be handed over, until the agreed payment terms are met.

5. Revisions and scope changes

Each project includes a reasonable number of revisions as agreed at the start. Requests that go beyond the agreed scope may require additional time and cost, which we'll discuss with you before proceeding.

6. Timelines

Project timelines depend on the agreed scope and on how quickly content, feedback and approvals are provided by the client. Delays on the client's side may extend the delivery timeline accordingly.

Once a project is paid for in full, ownership of the final deliverables (the website files, or the automation workflow built for you) transfers to you, unless otherwise agreed in writing. 9. No guaranteed business outcomes

We build and deliver the agreed website or automation to a professional standard, but we don't guarantee specific business results (traffic, sales, leads, rankings) as these depend on many factors outside our control.

10. Third-party services

Some projects may rely on third-party services (hosting, form providers, APIs, AI tools). We aren't responsible for outages, pricing changes or policy changes made by those third parties.
