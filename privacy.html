Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This is a general privacy policy template for a small digital studio. It is provided as a starting point, not legal advice — have a qualified professional review it against applicable privacy law (e.g. India's DPDP Act) before relying on it.

1. What we collect

When you use the enquiry form on the Contact page, we collect what you choose to submit: your name, business/company name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, what you need (website / AI automation / both / something else), budget range, and your project details.

2. Why we collect it

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, understand your project and, where relevant, prepare a quote. We don't use it for any other purpose.

3. How it's used

Enquiry details are used solely by Turing Point to communicate with you about your project. We don't sell, rent or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Form backend

Note for the site owner: at the time of writing, the Contact form is not yet connected to a live backend or third-party form service. Once it is (for example, Formspree or Web3Forms), this section should be updated to name that service and link to its own privacy policy, since your enquiry data would pass through it to reach us.

5. Cookies and local storage

This site does not currently use tracking cookies or analytics. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated to explain what's collected and why.

6. Data retention

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and, if we work together, for the duration of the project and any legally required record-keeping period afterwards.

7. Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by reaching out through the Contact page.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version on this page is the one that applies at any given time.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Contact page.
