Process

From Idea to Launch.

A clear, six-step process so you always know what's happening and what's next.

01

Discover

We start by understanding your business, your goals and what the project actually needs to achieve.

02

Plan

We scope the pages, features and timeline, so the project has a clear structure from day one.

03

Design

We shape the visual direction and user experience around your brand and your audience.

04

Build

We turn the approved design into a responsive, working digital product.

05

Test

We check the build across devices and browsers, and fix issues before anything goes live.

06

Launch

We deploy the finished experience and hand it over, ready to use.
